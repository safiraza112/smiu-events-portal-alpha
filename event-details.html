5 Dec 2026 Tech & Innovation

SMIU Annual Tech Fest 2026

Join SMIU's flagship technology showcase featuring competitive speed programming, web hackathons, robotics exhibitions, and industry tech talks.

Organized by the SMIU Computer Science Society in collaboration with the Student Affairs Office, Annual Tech Fest brings together students from all departments to solve real-world engineering challenges and network with visiting software engineers.

Event Information

  • 09:30 AM – 10:15 AM: Opening Ceremony & Keynote Welcome
  • 10:30 AM – 01:00 PM: Speed Programming & UI/UX Design Sprint
  • 01:00 PM – 02:00 PM: Lunch & Networking Break
  • 02:00 PM – 04:00 PM: Final-Year Project Exhibition & Judging
  • 04:15 PM – 05:00 PM: Shield Distribution & Closing Ceremony

  • Dr. Kamran Siddiqui — Head of Computer Science Department, SMIU
  • Ayesha Tariq — Senior Full-Stack Engineer, Systems Limited
  • Bilal Ahmed — Product Lead & Startup Mentor, NIC Karachi

  • All participants must carry their valid SMIU Student ID card.
  • Team competitions allow 2 to 4 members per team from any batch.
  • Use of pre-built templates or plagiarized code during live rounds results in disqualification.
  • Participants must report to the venue 20 minutes before their scheduled round.

Event Details

  • Date 5 December 2026
  • Time 9:30 AM – 5:00 PM
  • Venue Main Auditorium
  • Entry Fee Free for SMIU Students
